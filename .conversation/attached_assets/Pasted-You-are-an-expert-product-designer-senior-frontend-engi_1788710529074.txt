==================================================
IMPORTANT VTU ATTENDANCE LOGIC
==================================================

Implement VTU 2025 attendance logic.

Default minimum attendance requirement:

85%

Each course must be calculated separately.

Formula:

attendance_percentage =
(present_classes / total_conducted_classes) * 100

Example:

Present = 42
Total = 48

Attendance =
87.50%

Do NOT simply average the percentages of subjects.

Calculate attendance from actual class counts.

The system must distinguish:

- Present
- Absent
- Not yet conducted
- Cancelled
- Holiday
- Optional/other activity if needed

Cancelled/holiday classes must NOT increase the total conducted classes.

The default status system:

GREEN:
Attendance >= 85%

YELLOW / WARNING:
Attendance >= 80% and < 85%

RED:
Attendance < 80%

Also show:

"VTU minimum: 85%"

"Possible condonation zone: below 85%, subject to applicable VTU rules and approval"

Never tell the student that condonation is automatic.

Display a disclaimer:

"Attendance rules are based on the configured VTU 2025 regulation. Institutional implementation may vary. Verify official notices from VTU/your college."

Make the attendance threshold configurable from Settings.
